Meet Jellybean: Chubby raccoon gets stuck in Illinois sewer grate, prompting police rescue

ILLINOIS — A round little raccoon has captured hearts online after finding itself in an unusually tight spot: wedged in a sewer grate.

The raccoon, affectionately nicknamed Jellybean for this retelling, appears in photos lying belly-up between the metal bars of a roadside storm drain, looking remarkably calm about the whole predicament.

According to the social-media caption accompanying the photos, police in Zion, Illinois, were called to help free the animal. The images, credited in the post to the Zion Police Department, show the raccoon peering up through the grate before its reported rescue.

A very tight squeeze

One photo shows Jellybean's fluffy belly resting across the grate, with the animal's paws stretched toward the edges. Another captures the raccoon from the opposite side, its head tilted toward the pavement.

A new internet favorite

Whether Jellybean simply misjudged a narrow opening or was searching for a snack isn't known. But the photos offer a gentle reminder that wildlife can end up in unexpected places in urban neighborhoods.

For now, the raccoon has earned one distinction: an especially memorable nickname and a story that is hard not to smile at.
